# Direction: cinematic product, cut to "Ritual"

Route: taste-skill `cinematic-product`, with dark-luxe used for the light. The film is a stage production with one protagonist per beat. It is now rendered for real in Blender (EEVEE, GPU) and edited in Remotion.

## Light

- **The beam.** Every keychain shot is lit by a real spotlight just above the product, shining down through drifting haze.
  - The cone you see is light scattering in that haze, so it actually lights the product.
  - It pools on a glossy black floor and casts real shadows.
  - In the hook it flickers on, on the first beat.
- **Key and rims.** A front key light shapes the face. Cool rim lights from behind separate dark bodies from the stage; light linking keeps them off the floor.
- **Exposure.** The dark stage runs at −2.3 EV so the PLA colours match the catalog: Matte Red is red, Matte Black is black.
- **Headlights.** They are emissive and bloom in the compositor, together with fog glow, two-point anamorphic streaks, a warm/cool grade and a vignette. That is the "aura".

## Shots

- **Hook.** The keychain drops into the beam on its chain. The chain is a verlet rope, re-simulated from frame 0 so every frame is deterministic.
- **Detail.** A macro shot at f/1.8, with a focus pull from the light signature to the grille while a grazing light rakes across the relief.
- **Flip.** The keychain turns to its carbon back and a narrow light sweeps across the weave.
- **Garage.** Twelve cars on eighth notes.
- **Wall.** A warm plaster wall under a lamp. A modelled split ring with a nickel and a brass key swings onto a real hook arm and settles. The camera then pans past the M5 and the G80 holders.
- **Talon.** The spinner spins up with real motion blur, then stops with its ring hanging straight down.

## Edit

- **Music.** The edit is cut to "Ritual" by BalloonPlanet (Artlist licence).
  - It runs at about 95 BPM; the drop lands at 1:34.69.
  - The 30 s films start four bars before the drop, so the hook and the detail macro play over the breakdown. The headlights switch on the drop, then there is one idea per bar.
  - The ads and the cut-down start one bar before the drop. In the wall ad the keys land on the hook exactly on the drop.
- **On the drop.** A warm flash and a light leak, then a small zoom punch on every beat after it.
- **Type.**
  - Michroma, uppercase.
  - Each character resolves out of a soft blur with a letter-spacing settle and a faint warm glow, then drifts up into blur on exit.
  - One easing family throughout.
- **Finish.** Grain and a vignette sit on top of everything.
- **Claims.** Every number on screen comes from `site/catalog/launch.json`. No car-maker logos or names are shown.
