# Grille Talk: first Meta (Facebook + Instagram) campaign

Images: `ads/out/*.png`. Ad text: `ads/COPY.md` (paste as written, character counts already fit Meta's limits).
Store facts used here: keychains $6.99-$9.49, wall key holders from $14.99, spinners $9.99, free shipping over $35,
US only, LAUNCH15 (15% off, one use, ends Oct 20), Buy 3 keychains get 1 free, wall holder + keychain 20% off.

## Before launch (one time)

1. Facebook Page for Grille Talk, Instagram linked to it.
2. Shopify: Sales channels > Facebook & Instagram app (Meta). Connect the Page, turn on the Pixel (data sharing:
   Maximum) and sync the catalog.
3. Meta Events Manager > your Pixel > Test events: open grilletalk.shop, view a product, add to cart. All three events
   should show up. Do not place a real order just to test.
4. Ads Manager: ad account in USD, payment method added (by the account owner, 18+).
5. Work out your break-even cost per purchase: average order value minus print cost, mailer, label and Shopify fees.
   This is the most an ad can cost per sale before you lose money. Write it down; every decision below uses it.

## Rules for the first two weeks

- Days 1-3: change nothing. Meta is still learning.
- Day 4: turn off any ad with no link clicks after $8 spent, or CTR (link) under 0.7% after 1,500 impressions.
- Day 7: look at cost per purchase.
  - Under your break-even: raise the budget 20% every 3 days, never more at once.
  - Above it but getting add-to-carts: keep running, add the retargeting campaign.
  - No add-to-carts at all after ~$50: the problem is the creative or the offer, not the targeting. Make new
    creative (short phone videos of the keychain on real keys, the color options, the spinner spinning) before
    spending more.
- Never edit a running ad's text or image: duplicate it and change the copy instead (editing resets learning).

## Things to avoid

- No car-maker logos anywhere (ads, profile picture, comments).
- Lead with the idea ("your car's face as a keychain"); keep model names in the image or the product page.
  Car makers can file trademark complaints that get ads removed.
- Keep "independent maker, not affiliated with any vehicle manufacturer" on the site and in the Page's About text.
- Don't promise delivery dates: the site says printed in 3-5 business days, then shipping time.
